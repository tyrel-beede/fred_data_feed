This note accompanies the Cross-country Historical Adoption of Technology (CHAT) dataset. 
CHAT is an unbalanced panel dataset with information on the adoption of over 100 technologies 
in more than 150 countries since 1800. We discuss the main aim of CHAT, its scope and limitations, 
as well as several ways in which we have used the data so far and ways to potentially 
use the data for other research.
